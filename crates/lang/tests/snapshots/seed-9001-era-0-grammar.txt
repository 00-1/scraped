  moods marked on the verb: optative (may…), conditional (would…), question; commands have no subject
  unmarked: singular, subject case, non-past, positive
  adjectives, numerals, 'this' and 'here' never inflect
  a counted noun is plural when the number is above one
  derivations sit next to the root, before inflection: AGT one who does it, PLACE where it is done, INSTR what it is done with (from verbs); ABST the state or quality (from nouns and adjectives); DIM a small one; ADJZ like it (from nouns)
  compounds: modifier then head (river-stone: a stone of the river)
  names: two words compounded (a quality or thing, then a beast or thing)

SYNTAX
  clause order: SOV
  dative (beneficiary): just before the object
  adverbs: just before the verb
  commands: plain non-past verb with no subject
  noun phrase: noun – adjectives – number – this
  possessor before possessed noun
  appositions (titles, 'child of X') follow the name
  'that' after the reported clause
  adverbial clauses (when…, because…) after the main clause; the linking word stands between them
  relative clauses before their noun; the relative word, in the case of the missing part, closes the clause on the far side
  more / most / as after their adjective
  the compared-with phrase stands outside the adjectives, on the side the adjectives are, with 'than' or 'like' between
  clauses of equal standing: and, but, or, then between them
  reported speech: that-clause where the object goes; quotations framed by opening and closing words

NUMERALS
  base: duodecimal (dozen, gross)
  order: biggest part first
  one dozen: 'dozen' alone
  linker between parts: no
  numeral signs: positional (one digit per place, with zero)
