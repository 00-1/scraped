# D09 samples: notes

The curious explorer for 10 hours on seeds 1, 42 and 9001 (example text only).

- **Strangeness everywhere, never explained.** Old everyday spells now show on arrival in houses, gate-passages, catacombs, roads and open country: four to nine times in ten hours. Each response still carries at most one of them beside the usual few facts. Arrivals stay as short as in D08.
- **Evidence beside the weather.** On seed 9001 a cold road carries both the season's frost and "frost where it should not", next to a cue about wolves, where a spell keeps them off. Lights that nothing casts lie on a house and on a sacred way at night.
- **Mostly placeholder.** Every cue except light reads "Something about the X is not as it should be", because `spell.cue` has only its one example variant. Jb's variants, one per quality and direction, are what will turn these into evidence: no tracks where wolves should pass, a jar cold to the hand, a doorframe the eye slides off.
- **Naming the thing touched.** A cue names the kind of thing the spell acts on ("the wolf", "the foxglove") even when none is in sight. With real variants that should read as an absence or an oddity of the place, not as a statement about wolves.

What reads oddly: the explorer meets few conditional spells, since it rarely stays where they wake (lamps at night, alarms in tombs). Nothing in ten hours shows a mill turning without water or a thing that won't lift.
