# D05 samples: notes

The curious explorer's first ten hours on seeds 1, 42 and 9001. Regenerate with `scraped-lang samples D05 --hours 10`. Example text only.

- **Things with owners.** "A stone seal stamp. It bears a crowned lion in a plain border." The same household's things carry the same emblem (seed 42: a measuring cup and an earring both bear a twin hand).
- **Measures.** A weight shows the numeral sign it is marked with and how heavy it is against the smallest: "marked with a plain sign, a hook turned up… weighs about 8 of the smallest".
- **Containers.** The explorer opens caskets, pitchers and urns; most are empty, some hold a small thing ("Inside: a mirror").
- **Machines.** It works a counterweight in a great building and is told it runs; the other parts of the works wait elsewhere in the building.
- **Doors that won't move** now give their cause where there is one to see (a keyhole, a gate with no handle this side, a sill worn but closed today).

Not shown in ten hours: keys turning in their locks, caches found under floor stones (the explorer looks closer in about half the rooms), digging where an old map's cross is, and a calendar door on its day. The tests cover each.
