# D15 samples: notes

Each way of playing over a season on seed 42, kept well as the measurement is (`scraped-lang samples D15 --bot arcs`), condensed to the commands around each step of its own arc and the end.

- **Forager:** first food within the first hour, then a long walk to wooded ground by drinkable water. Shelter, fire and naming the place come together on day 16, then food laid up and `settle`. The end summary has a line for each way it went along.
- **Mason:** works a counterweight in the first hour (the first works part). A hidden way is found by looking closely, and later other parts move. It never settles: the building it keeps to is never seen whole, and the works it found are in great buildings far off.
- **Wayfarer:** reaches landmarks and a summit on the first day, sees the rim, then goes beyond. Arriving at the rim stops the walk, and only then does the game suggest going beyond.
