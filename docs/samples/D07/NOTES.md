# D07 samples: notes

Spoiler pages for seeds 1, 42 and 9001: names with their meanings, the grammar sheet's morphology and syntax, and longer sentences built from random meanings in the oldest and latest eras (commands on each page). The game's texts don't use the new grammar yet; D08 writes them.

- **Names mean something.** Seed 1's towns are mostly hills ("Liminuhiwaku", oak hill; "Walihiwaku", stone hill; "Hihuhiwaku", white hill), so "-hiwaku" is there to be noticed; kings are a quality and a beast ("good fox", "dark crow").
- **Every construction in one sentence.** Seed 42's first sentence holds a potent wish, a comparison ("more … than"), a superlative, a direct quotation with a potent clause inside and a conditional joined by "or". Each linking word, quote mark and comparison word is its own word; derivations and compounds gloss as `flower+adjz`, `island~gate`.
- **Eras.** Seed 9001's latest era wears the words down (`polgatato'o` → `pòlgëtëdhò`) but keeps the structure word for word.

What reads oddly: random meanings make nonsense ("the fish+absts rule the most seal+adjz oil+abst"). That is expected here, and D08's texts will have real content. The English glosses are spoiler-only and rough in places ("have lay").
