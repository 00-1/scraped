**> go 1 intact house**

```
A hall, dim and close. A stone hearth stands here. Ways out: a door north (closed), a stair up and the way out.
```

**> examine stone hearth**

```
A stone hearth. Marks are cut into it: writing. You could read it.
```

**> read stone hearth**

```
On a stone hearth, thirty-four signs in eight groups, about three lines, in a slanted hand. A few shapes keep coming back.

Dust hides some of the signs.
```
