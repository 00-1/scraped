# D13: the wayfarer, seed 42

7 commands, 3.4 hours of game time. Example text only; spoiler-free.

```
You wake in a grassland, with no memory of arriving.

A worn holy city of a few houses. The intact observatory stands alone of its kind here. The hilltop rock pillar to the southeast (far).

Before this, were you a scribe, a forager, a mason or a wayfarer? Or none of these.
```

**> wayfarer**

```
Your eye goes to the lie of the land and the sky.

The worn temple stands alone of its kind here. The road runs here.
```

**> look up**

```
The sky: sun low in the east. Birds wheel overhead.
```

**> look around**

```
A holy city by a hilltop, clustered. Parts: temple quarter, sacred way, scholars' quarter, graves and old town. A sinkhole to the south. Field walls to the southwest. Here and there: withered growth. Dark ash tree, in flower. A few tawny crows to the west, feeding.
```

**> go to the hilltop rock pillar**

```
You travel southeast, most of a morning's walk.

You stop: the damaged waystation has come into view to the southwest.

The plain hermitage to the north (middle). Here and there: bare earth.

You could go back.
```

**> look up**

```
The sky: sun low in the east. Birds wheel overhead.
```

**> look around**

```
Grassland, flat ground. The damaged waystation to the southwest (middle). Here and there: cracked mud. You notice young animals. Bright olive, in flower. One blue moth to the northwest, at the flowers.

You could look down.
```

**> go to the damaged waystation**

```
You travel southwest, most of a morning's walk.

You reach the damaged waystation.

A damaged waystation stands alone here. Here and there: bare earth. You notice new leaves.

You could go back.
```
