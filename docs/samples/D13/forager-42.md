# D13: the forager, seed 42

10 commands, 3.6 hours of game time. Example text only; spoiler-free.

```
You wake in a grassland, with no memory of arriving.

A worn holy city of a few houses. The intact observatory stands alone of its kind here. The hilltop rock pillar to the southeast (far).

Before this, were you a scribe, a forager, a mason or a wayfarer? Or none of these.
```

**> forager**

```
Your eye goes to what grows and moves.

The worn temple stands alone of its kind here. The road runs here. Chewed leaves, fresh.
```

**> forage**

```
You find nothing to eat.
```

**> listen**

```
Faintly, a chatter from the south. Faintly, river from the southeast.
```

**> smell**

```
A smell of flowers.
```

**> look**

```
The square. A smell of flowers. Spawn, fresh. Faintly, a chatter from the south.
```

**> look around**

```
A holy city by a hilltop, clustered. Parts: temple quarter, sacred way, scholars' quarter, graves and old town. A sinkhole to the south. Field walls to the southwest. Dark ash tree, in flower. One green locust to the southeast, on the wing. Silvery gorse, in leaf.
```

**> look up**

```
The sky: sun low in the east. Birds wheel overhead.
```

**> wait**

```
You wait a while. One blue moth to the east, on the wing.
```

**> examine 1 stone carving**

```
A stone carving. Marks are cut into it.
```

**> go to the hilltop rock pillar**

```
You travel southeast, most of a morning's walk.

You stop: the damaged waystation has come into view to the southwest.

Here and there: bare earth. Runs in the grass.
```
