# D13 samples: notes

One hour of each style bot on seed 42, from the same start (`scraped-lang samples D13 --bot styles`).

- The answer to the offer is the first command. Each style's line is the example variant of `style.chosen`.
- After that, the four diverge at once in what arrives unasked. The forager's first two responses add animal signs ("Chewed leaves, fresh.", "Spawn, fresh."). The wayfarer's look and look around add the ground ("withered growth", "sodden ground"). The mason feels the forecourt ("Something in the stone here is not right", a spell's sign in its category). The scribe's look is the plainest.
- The bots' own commands are mostly the explorer's in the first hour; the style's own attention takes about a quarter of later steps.
