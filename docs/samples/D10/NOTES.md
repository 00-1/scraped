The curious explorer for 10 hours on seeds 1, 42 and 9001 (example text only).

- **Cleaning is ordinary.** The explorer rubs moss, lichen, soot and dust off what it reads, five to eight times in ten hours, then reads on. Nothing in the reply says why it would matter.
- **A knife changes it.** Once it carries a knife, "clean" scrapes the whole top layer away ("You scrape a stone wall clean"), and the signs' sounds come with it. That is the first hint that writing does something, and it comes from an ordinary act, hours in.
- **Nothing in the first hour.** No reply in the first hour of any seed scrapes, writes, or speaks of writing's force. A great site's air is now felt ("heavy and still"), not named.
- **Strangeness stays unexplained.** Doors, jars, boxes, snakes and wolves "not as it should be"; frost where it should not lie. These still read as placeholders until Jb writes `spell.cue`'s variants.

What reads oddly: grime hides signs on many surfaces at once, so cleaning becomes routine and loses its surprise. None of the three runs reaches the rim in ten hours, so the transcripts don't show the way out.
