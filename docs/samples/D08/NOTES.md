# D08 samples: notes

Spoiler pages for seeds 1, 42 and 9001: each world's first dozen stories, with every text that tells part of one, where it lies (town and building), its genre, era and year, and the English gloss.

- **A story told in many places.** Seed 42's earthquake (arc 5) is in nine texts in eight places: the annal in Hikumime's archive, a prayer in its temple, the empty jars counted in the storehouse, the rebuilt gate's inscription, the stone and oil for it in the market hall, and the king's decree against selling fish set up in four towns.
- **The same theft from three sides.** Seed 42's first trial is in an annal, a court record that quotes both parties ("Rekïdongu did not steal the fish"), and a curse scratched in the victim's house.
- **Works and those who gave up on them.** Projects end in a building inscription and a storekeeper's tally, or in the builder's letter to the king that the men will not build.

What reads oddly: the gloss shows every derived word raw ("build+agt" for builder) and some words of colour land strangely ("each small ox", "in the 19th year … again"); these come from varying each event's wording. Stories of lives are thin (birth, appointment, a deed): the texts about one person are mostly others' stories they appear in.
