# D04 samples: notes

The curious explorer's first four hours inside the world's largest great interior, on seeds 1, 42 and 9001, starting at its entrance with a lamp. Regenerate with `scraped-lang samples D04 --hours 4 --inside`. Example text only.

- **Palaces at scale.** Seed 42's palace runs gate-court, throne-room, residence, refectory, kitchens, baths, dormitories off long corridors; seed 1 opens on a vast gate-court ("your steps echoing").
- **Mapping by the text.** Every move says which way and how far ("North by the door, some 10 metres"). Where several doors face one way they are told apart by name ("the western door north"), and the explorer works through them one by one.
- **Finding the way.** It marks the entrance ("You scratch a mark on the wall") and follows long passages in one go ("The passage runs on west, some 24 metres, past three turnings").
- **Brief rooms.** A room is said in about three facts: the space, what stands out, the ways out. More comes from `look closer`.

Not shown in these four hours: walking back to a known space by name (the explorer stays three hours before leaving), bad air (deep mines and caves) and the hidden spaces (the explorer looks closer in about half the rooms; the mapper finds them all).
