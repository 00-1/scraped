# Playtest 2026-10-11 (second)

Jb's second playtest, the same evening, with an AI partner over MCP, on v0.6.0 (after S06). About 370 commands: the start town's storehouse and tally-room, a walk east to a burial mound and a waterfall and back, the temple felt through in the dark and then swept by day, and a walk northeast to a waystation.

- `transcript.md`: every command and the game's text, verbatim.
- `save.txt`: the sealed save from the end of the session.
- `field-book.html` and `field-book-data/`: the field book the players kept. It's a pannable map, places, items, inscriptions, a sign list, notes, and a "world" tab: season, weather, time and history, each guessed from quoted evidence.

What it showed is in `docs/LOG.md` (2026-10-11, second playtest) and `docs/milestones/S07-from-the-second-playtest.md`. The field book is the second example of how players organise evidence (see X01).
