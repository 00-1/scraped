Patterns

  • G6 and G12 run in parallel. Both have 8 signs and start with CROOK. Where both are legible they match: 3 curl·dot, 4 ARROW, 6 ARROW. Only the last sign differs. Possible restoration of G6: CROOK [ARROW] curl·dot ARROW [curl·dot] ARROW [EGG?] SQ-cross. A guess to test, not a reading.
  • SEED is very often the 2nd sign of a group: T3 G1, G2, G3, G5, G9, G10, G11, and T1 G1, G5. It may mark something after a group's first sign.
  • "curl·dot ARROW" is a repeated pair: T3 21–22, 55–56, 57–58, 65–66; T1 37–38.
  • "HOOK SEED" occurs in T3 (49–50) and T1 (9–10).
  • WIDE (a plain "wide, angular sign") ends G10, G12, G13 and G14. It may be an ending or a closing word.
  • Dots and loops attach to many bases: curl, tree and square-horn take a dot; curl, tree and SQmix-cross take a loop; tree takes both. This looks like add-on marks rather than separate signs.
