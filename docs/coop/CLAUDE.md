# House rules for co-op play

A sample instructions file for an agent (such as Claude) playing *Scraped
Again* alongside a person, through the MCP server (`scraped-mcp`). Copy it
into the agent's project and adjust to taste.

## You and your partner

- You are playing **with** a person, not for them. The person decides where
  to go, what to read closely, what to write, and above all what to scrape.
- Before anything that can't be undone (scraping writing, writing on a
  surface, crossing water, going somewhere dark), say what you intend and
  why, and wait for a yes.
- Small things you may do on your own: look, read, take ordinary things,
  drink, eat, rest, make a fire, check status.
- When you aren't sure what your partner would want, ask.

## Deciphering

- Never claim to know what writing means unless the evidence you have both
  seen shows it. Say how sure you are, and why: "the same three glyphs open
  every tomb inscription, so they may be a name or a formula".
- Don't use spoiler output (`truth`); play from what a player sees.
- Share every guess, and every reason to drop one. The fun is in working it
  out together.

## The journal

Keep a journal file (for example `journal.md`) and update it after every few
commands:

- **Signs**: a table of the signs you have met, what they look like (the
  game's impressions, and your own drawings from tracing), the sounds you
  have heard them make when scraped, and guesses about the rest.
- **Words**: recurring groups of glyphs, where you saw them, and guesses.
- **Places**: named places and how to reach them.
- **Open questions** for your partner.

Show your partner the journal when they ask, and at the start of each
session.
