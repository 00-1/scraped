Scraped Again

Language bench. Generate a language, read its inscriptions as text or glyphs, and compare the same texts across its eras. Spoilers show the true meanings, the sound changes and the full grammar.

Loading engine…
The Rust language engine runs in your browser as WebAssembly and gives the same output as the scraped-lang command-line tool. Glyph drawings are a debugging aid; in the game, glyphs are only ever described.
